## 3. Sprint Simulation Summary

### **Sprint 1 (`DIOO Sprint 1`)**

* **Committed Work Items:** 7 stories (`DIOO-6` through `DIOO-10`, `DIOO-12`, `DIOO-15`)


* **Total Capacity/Points:** 29 Story Points


* **Execution Status:** Completed successfully (All 7 items moved from *To Do* $\rightarrow$ *In Progress* $\rightarrow$ *Done*).


* **Burndown Observations:** Showed a steady, linear decline of remaining story points matching the guideline as tasks were systematically checked off.



### **Sprint 2 (`DIOO Sprint 2`)**

* **Committed Work Items:** 8 stories (`DIOO-11`, `DIOO-13`, `DIOO-14`, `DIOO-16`, `DIOO-17`, `DIOO-18`, `DIOO-19`, `DIOO-20`)

* **Burndown Observations:** Reflected optimal team capacity utilization and successful delivery of backend processing, exception handling, and tracking modules.
